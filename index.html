ABOUT ME

I am Kizito Cosmos, a videographer, graphic designer, and Salesian animator passionate about using digital media to tell meaningful stories, connect communities, and communicate messages of faith and hope.

My experience in video editing, graphic design, and event coverage has given me opportunities to document experiences, support communication initiatives, and create visual content that engages audiences.

As someone involved in youth ministry, I am particularly interested in exploring how storytelling, journalism, and digital media can help young people encounter Christ, share their experiences, and become active participants in the mission of the Church.

I continue to develop my creative skills with a desire to use media not only as a profession, but also as a tool for evangelization and service.
